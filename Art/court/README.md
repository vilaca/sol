# Court card art

The J, Q and K figures are the English-pattern playing cards by Dmitry Fomin, from Wikimedia Commons
([category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_English_pattern_playing_cards),
e.g. [king of hearts](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:English_pattern_king_of_hearts.svg)),
released under the [CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
No attribution is required; we give it anyway.

The SVGs here are unmodified. `scripts/make-court-art.py` crops each figure and renders the
`court-*.imageset` PNGs in `App/Assets.xcassets` (needs `brew install librsvg`).
